🔒 Privacy Policy

Pi Testnet SaaS Platform Testnet

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Pi Testnet SaaS Platform ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our decentralized application (dApp) built on the Pi Network testnet.

By using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Pi Wallet Address: Your public Pi Network wallet address for transactions and authentication
  • Transaction Data: Information about your transactions on the Pi testnet
  • Service Usage: Which services you access and how you interact with our platform

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform, including pages visited and features used
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Network Information: IP address and network connection details
📝 Note: We only collect information that is necessary for the operation of our services on the Pi testnet. We do not collect sensitive personal information like names, email addresses, or physical addresses unless explicitly provided by you.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our SaaS platform
  • Transaction Processing: To facilitate and verify transactions on the Pi testnet
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our platform and improve user experience
  • Security: To monitor for and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities
  • Communications: To send important updates about our services (only with your consent)

4. Blockchain Data

As a decentralized application on the Pi Network, certain information is inherently public on the blockchain:

  • Public Wallet Addresses: Your Pi wallet address is visible on the testnet blockchain
  • Transaction History: All transactions are recorded on the public testnet ledger
  • Smart Contract Interactions: Your interactions with our smart contracts are publicly verifiable

Important: We do not control the blockchain. Any information you submit to the blockchain is permanently public and cannot be deleted.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Off-Chain Storage: Some data may be stored on our secure servers (e.g., analytics data)
  • Encryption: We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to internal data systems
  • Data Retention: We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services
🔒 Security Best Practices: We recommend that you:
  • Keep your Pi wallet private key secure
  • Never share your private key with anyone
  • Use a secure connection when accessing our platform

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to enhance our platform:

  • Pi Network SDK: For blockchain integration
  • Analytics Tools: To understand platform usage patterns
  • Infrastructure Providers: For hosting and data storage

These third parties have their own privacy policies and data practices. We encourage you to review them.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data we hold
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your off-chain data
  • Opt-Out: Opt-out of non-essential data collection

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@saops.masavi.site

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • Providing prominent notice on our platform

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
